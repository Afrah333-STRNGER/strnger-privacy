  • AI-generated responses may be inaccurate, generic, or inappropriate to your specific situation, and must not be relied upon for medical, legal, psychological, or emergency guidance
  • If your words suggest a possible crisis, the App may automatically display crisis resources. This is a best-effort, automated feature — not a monitored or guaranteed intervention. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately; do not rely on this feature to reach help on your behalf
