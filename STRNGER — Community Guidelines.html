STRNGER — Community Guidelines

Effective Date: March 22, 2026

Last updated: March 26, 2026

Our Purpose

STRNGER is a space for anonymous emotional support, reflection, and kindness.

Everyone here is human. Everyone here is going through something.
Treat others the way you would want to be treated on your hardest day.

1. Be Kind & Respectful

You must always:

●     Speak with empathy and understanding

●     Respect different experiences and emotions

●     Be supportive, not judgmental

Not allowed:

●     Hate speech or discrimination of any kind

●     Mocking someone’s feelings or struggles

●     Harass, bullying, or harm other users

●     Share harmful, abusive, or illegal content

●     Attempt to exploit, hack, or disrupt the App

●     Share personal contact details (phone numbers, emails, social media)

●     Impersonate others

●     Use the app for commercial or spam purposes

 

 2. Protect Anonymity (Very Important)

STRNGER is built on anonymity — do not break it.

You must NOT:

●     Share your real name

●     Share phone numbers, emails, or social media

●     Ask others for personal information

●     Try to identify or track other users

 Messages containing personal contact info may be automatically blocked.

3. Keep Conversations Safe

This is a support space, not a harmful one.

Not allowed:

●     Sexual content or explicit messages

●     Graphic or disturbing content

●     Encouraging self-harm or harmful behavior

●     Threats or intimidation

4. Mental Health Responsibility

STRNGER is peer support only, not professional care.

●     Do not present yourself as a therapist or expert

●     Do not give medical or clinical advice

●     Avoid telling others what they must do

Instead:

●     Share gently

●     Suggest seeking professional help when needed

●     Be present, not authoritative

5. Crisis Situations

If someone seems in danger:

●     Encourage them to seek real-world help

●     Do NOT try to handle serious crises yourself

STRNGER is not equipped for emergencies.

6. No Abuse or Misuse

You must NOT:

●     Spam or send repetitive messages

●     Manipulate, deceive, or exploit others

●     Use the app for advertising or promotion

●     Attempt to hack, reverse engineer, or abuse the system

7. Reporting & Enforcement

We take safety seriously.

If you break these guidelines:

●     Your messages may be blocked

●     Your account may be suspended

●     Repeated violations may result in a permanent ban

Users can report harmful behavior, and accounts with multiple reports are reviewed.

8. Respect Emotional Boundaries

Everyone processes things differently.

●     Don’t pressure others to share more than they want

●     Don’t invalidate someone’s feelings

●     It’s okay to step away from a conversation

9. Use STRNGER the Right Way

This app is for:

●     Emotional support

●     Anonymous conversations

●     Personal reflection

This app is not for:

●     Dating

●     Networking

●     Self-promotion

Final Reminder

Behind every message is a real person.

Be the reason someone feels:

●     Heard

●     Safe

●     A little less alone
